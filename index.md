# Privacy Policy — Sleep Early!

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

Sleep Early! is a habit-support app. It is not a medical device.

## What we collect
- Your sleep schedule: target bedtime, wake time, workdays, timezone.
- Your nightly logs: bedtime, fall-asleep and wake times, what kept you up (the barrier you pick), whether you completed the suggested action, and an optional mood rating.
- An anonymous account ID. If you choose to link an email, that email address.

## How it's used
To show your streak and trends, and to send the reminders you turned on. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for ads.

## Where it's stored
In our Supabase database, protected by row-level security so only your account can read your rows. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## Your controls
- **Export**: Settings → Export my data.
- **Delete**: Settings → Delete my account & data permanently removes your profile and all logs.
- Notifications can be turned off any time in Settings or your phone's system settings.
